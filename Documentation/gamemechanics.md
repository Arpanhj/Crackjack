
# GAME MECHANICS

## Base game

Every player holds three hands, consisting of, initially, two cards each. The goal is similar to that of blackjack: the player aims to reach a value of 21 per hand, ideally using two cards (A (holding a value of 11) and any of 10, J, Q, K). However, if the player exceeds that number, the hand is a "bust". It is worth nothing, and can not be used to win against other cards with. Every non-bust hand wins over busts.
In accordance with blackjack rules, once per hand per "lap" around the table, the player may hit or stand.
When every player has decided to stand on all hands, all cards are revealed at the same time, and then all hands are played out against all other players' hands, 1v1. The hand with the larger value (while not busting) wins, acquiring a point for the player. Plays where both hands are of equal value yield no points.
Splitting is not a game mechanic in crackjack.
